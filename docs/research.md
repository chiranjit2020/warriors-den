## 3. UX Problems (current Warriors Den site)

- Single scrolling page with anchor nav — no crawlable, linkable URLs per program or location, which caps SEO ceiling and makes deep-linking (e.g., from ads or social) impossible.
- No visible schedule, pricing, or "what happens in a trial class" information — high-friction for a first-time visitor trying to decide.
- Founders shown as images with no bios/credentials — trust gap for the parent persona in particular (safety/coach-quality concerns).
- No testimonials, reviews, or third-party trust signals surfaced on-site, despite Google reviews almost certainly existing (business is active and has a Google Business Profile per the master prompt).
- Gallery images are unlabeled/uncaptioned — no way to tell which program or location a photo represents.
- Only one physical location shown despite the business apparently operating more sites (per the Sanshinkan affiliate finding above).
- No clear single primary CTA — "Reach out to us today" is generic, not action/outcome-specific (e.g., a free trial).

## 4. SEO Problems

- Anchor-based single page = one indexable URL for the entire site; each of the 7 programs and any location competes for the same page instead of ranking independently for terms like "Muay Thai classes Kolkata" or "MMA classes Kolkata."
- No evidence of per-page titles/meta descriptions, structured data, sitemap, or robots.txt.
- No blog/content layer, so no way to capture informational search intent (e.g., "benefits of martial arts for kids," "self-defence classes for women Kolkata").
- No internal linking structure beyond the single anchor nav.

## 5. Content Problems

- Program listings are names only — no outcomes, audience fit, or benefits copy, which the master prompt explicitly calls for.
- No stated audience/age guidance per program.
- No differentiation of Warriors Den from a generic local gym in the copy.
- "One-year anniversary" framing means marketing copy should lean on genuine current strengths (coaching, environment, community) rather than implied decades of legacy.

## 6. Conversion Problems

- No dedicated trial-booking flow or form; only a generic contact form.
- No phone/WhatsApp click-to-action treatment described.
- No location-specific "get directions" or map embed identified.
- No urgency/next-step guidance after contact form submission beyond a generic success message.

## 7. Opportunities

- Clear content gap Warriors Den can win: outcome-focused, locally-specific program pages (7 disciplines × Kolkata search intent) that the current single-page site cannot support.
- Sanshinkan's own site demonstrates the parent org already has a working content/trust playbook (named instructor pages, Google Reviews block, video showcase, downloadable schedules) that can be locally adapted — instructions, not copied wholesale, per the master prompt.
- A real "Free Trial" flow (form + WhatsApp + call) would materially close the biggest conversion gap on the current site.
- Multi-location architecture (if confirmed) is a strong local-SEO opportunity once addresses are verified.
